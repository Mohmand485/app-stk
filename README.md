# app-stk

Password-protected live A-share watchlist: daily signals, history, outlook and backtest.
Open https://mohmand485.github.io/app-stk/ and sign in.

- Data loads live from Eastmoney each time you open the page or tap Refresh.
- Add stocks by code, name or pinyin. Your watchlist and holdings are saved on your own device.
- The page is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and can't be read without the login.

For information only. This is not investment advice. Signals are alerts; you place every trade yourself.
